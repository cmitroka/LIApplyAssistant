About the job
Job Description:

Position Overview

The primary responsibility of the Director of AI-First Development is to lead the teams that design, build, and maintain software applications and integrations for Las Vegas Sands. The role is accountable for delivering secure, reliable, and well-engineered solutions that support the company’s business objectives.

The Director translates business priorities into delivery plans, sets engineering standards and practices, and continuously improves how the department builds and ships software, including the responsible adoption of AI-assisted development tools.

This role operates within an AI-First Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC) in which AI agents serve as primary producers of code, configuration, and test artifacts, while the Director leads and directs a pod of AI-First Software Engineers, DevOps Engineers, AppSec Engineers and partners closely with Enterprise Architecture, Cyber Security, and Infrastructure teams.

All duties are to be performed in accordance with departmental and Las Vegas Sands Corp.’s policies, practices, and procedures. All Las Vegas Sands Corp. Team Members are expected to conduct and carry themselves in a professional manner at all times. Team Members are required to observe the company’s standards, work requirements and rules of conduct.

Essential Duties & Responsibilities

Own the delivery of the team’s software portfolio, translating business priorities into roadmaps, release plans, and capacity-based team plans.
Work with business leaders, project managers and business analysts to understand the business goals, processes, and pain points, and provide transparency into priorities, progress, and trade-offs.
Manage dependencies, risks, and issues across teams, vendors, and partner technology groups, escalating decisions when needed.
Report delivery status, metrics, and forecasts to PMO and senior IT leadership.
Set and maintain engineering standards for code quality, testing, code review, documentation, and secure development.
Drive adoption of AI first development, Agile, Continuous Integration/Continuous Delivery, DevOps, and automated testing practices across the teams.
Lead the responsible adoption of AI-assisted development tools, including the guardrails, review practices, and measures needed to use them safely and effectively.
Track delivery and quality metrics such as lead time, deployment frequency, change failure rate, and escaped defects, and use them to improve how teams work.
Partner with Enterprise Architecture on solution designs and technical direction, and manage technical debt so applications stay current, supportable, and highly available.
Hire, onboard, coach, and manage the performance of team members.
Develop leadership and technical talent in the team and plan succession for key roles.
Build strong, productive, and cohesive team with a culture of accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement.
Forecast staffing and skills needs based on priorities.
Ensure solutions meet information security, privacy, and regulatory requirements, including PCI DSS, SOX, and gaming regulations where applicable.
Lead and support audit and compliance reviews for the team’s applications.
Perform job duties in a safe manner.
Attend work as scheduled on a consistent and regular basis.
Perform other related duties as assigned.


Minimum Qualifications

At least 21 years of age.
Proof of authorization to work in the United States.
Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related field required; graduate degree preferred.
Must be able to obtain and maintain any certification or license, as required by law or policy.
12+ years of software development experience, including at least 5 years managing software development teams and 2 years leading managers. Hospitality, gaming, or casino industry experience preferred.
Proven track record delivering enterprise software applications and integrations that are highly available, scalable, and secure.
Strong hands-on technical background in modern software development, including cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure), APIs and microservices, relational and NoSQL databases, and common languages (e.g., Java, C#, Python, JavaScript/TypeScript).
Strong working knowledge of Agile and Lean delivery, CI/CD, DevOps, automated testing, and secure development practices (e.g., OWASP, SAST, DAST).
Experience introducing AI-assisted development tools into engineering teams preferred.
Experience working in a regulated environment (e.g., PCI DSS, SOX, gaming regulation).
Demonstrated ability to manage budgets, forecasts, and third-party vendors.
Excellent communication skills, with the ability to explain technical topics to business leaders and influence decisions across the organization.
Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to communicate effectively and interact appropriately with management, other Team Members and outside contacts of different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Ability to travel, including internationally.
Must be able to work varied shifts, including nights, weekends and holidays.

Competitors

Las Vegas Sands is a leading global developer and operator of large-scale integrated resorts, with a portfolio concentrated in Macau (multiple Cotai Strip properties) and Singapore (Marina Bay Sands). Industry reports highlight that large operators benefit from scale, capital access, and sophisticated operations, while facing pressures from regulatory changes, license renewals, and evolving consumer preferences.
